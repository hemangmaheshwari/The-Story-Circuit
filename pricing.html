Frequently Asked Questions

How do monthly retainer packages work?

Once subscribed, you will be onboarded to our collaboration board (Trello/Slack). You can add creative briefs directly, and we deliver finished video/graphic items on a rolling basis. Revisions are completed quickly so your content schedule never skips a beat.

What files do we receive?

For video deliverables, you get color-graded, sound-designed 1080p MP4 master files, ready for upload. For design projects, we provide web-optimized PNG/JPG files along with editable Figma or Adobe source files upon request.

Can we pause or cancel our subscription?

Yes! You can pause or cancel your monthly plan at any time. If you pause, any unused days in your billing cycle will be saved for when you resume creative sprints.
